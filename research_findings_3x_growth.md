## 1. Base rates: 3X is rare-but-normal, not a lottery

From the same 4,971-ticker / 2015–2026 monthly backtest (survivorship-biased upper bounds):

| Starting bucket | **3X / 3yr** | 5X / 3yr | 10X / 3yr |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| All stocks | 11.6% | 4.6% | 1.4% |
| **Price ≥ $5 (investable)** | **8.4% (~1 in 12)** | 2.6% | 0.6% |
| Price $1–5 | 29.7% | 14.9% | 4.9% |

- Cohort dispersion is large (2020 cohort: ~12%; 2021–22 cohorts: ~5%) — regime matters and can't be timed.
- **Winner profile relaxes vs 10X:** 3X winners include plenty of $5–20B names; the hard mcap gate
  moves from ~$2B (10X) to ~$20B (3X). A 3X only asks a $10B company to reach $30B — precedented
  in every one of our sectors.

**Theme-universe precedent (the "has this been seen before" answer): yes, emphatically.**
71 of 119 theme tickers 3X'd from a $5+ start in some 36-month window 2015–2024 — NTRA did it in 7
separate cohort windows, MOD in 6, STRL in 5, FIX/POWL/AAOI/CRSP/VEEV in 4, LEU/VST/NET in 3.
These themes are *built* from the kind of stocks that 3X.

## 2. The 3X framework (what changes vs 10X)

1. **Mcap ceiling ~$20B** (was ~$2–5B). 3X targets must stay below sector all-time peaks — this now
   passes many names the 10X test killed (TEM→$33B ok; MP→$28B stretch-ok; TLN→$54B < CEG today).
2. **One engine is enough** (was two): EITHER ≥25–35% growth with operating leverage, OR a credible
   re-rate from a depressed multiple (deep drawdown in an intact theme), OR a binary catalyst where a
   *partial* win re-rates 2–4×.
3. **Dilution still kills** — a company doubling its share count needs a 6X enterprise for a 3X per
   share (excludes UUUU despite great policy tailwinds).
4. **Already-parabolic still excluded** — MYRG (+137%/1y), TWST (+180%), GH/NTRA (~$20B after their
   runs) spent their re-rate.
