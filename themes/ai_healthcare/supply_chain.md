# AI in Healthcare — Supply Chain & Second-Order Map

**Structure:** Long-only, quality-weighted toward incumbency and real revenue today. All candidates US-listed common stock or liquid sponsored ADR (Fidelity-holdable). Pre-revenue names appear only in a labeled speculative sleeve.

**Organizing thesis:** AI is the commodity layer; the scarce, un-buyable asset is the *system of record* the AI must run inside — the EHR/CRM/trial workflow, the imaging + diagnostics installed base, and the proprietary molecular-data corpus. Weight toward the owners of that distribution; discount the pre-revenue story stocks.

## Bucket 1 — Clinical AI Workflow: Systems of Record (HIGH PRIORITY — LONG)
The bet: workflow AI (ambient scribing, coding automation, agentic CRM) is a margin/throughput upgrade that mostly accrues to whoever owns the record and the physician distribution it plugs into.

## Explicitly Excluded

- **NVDA (Nvidia)** — real and large healthcare/BioNeMo exposure, but too diffuse: healthcare is an un-attributable sliver of a GPU franchise driven by unrelated demand. Fails the "exposure must be legible in the income statement" test.
- **HIMS (Hims & Hers)** — consumer telehealth/DTC brand, not AI-into-legacy-workflow. Growth is marketing/fulfillment-driven, not an AI-on-systems-of-record bet. Out of scope.
- **ILMN (Illumina)** — sequencing hardware/consumables platform; a genomics-instrument story more than an AI story, and carrying company-specific overhangs. Watchlist, not core.
- **LH (Labcorp)** — redundant to DGX as a national-lab data owner; if the lab-scale sub-thesis works I'd rather express it once via DGX. Held in reserve.
- **CERT (Certara)** — legitimate biosimulation/model-informed drug development, but thinner and more services-like; parked as a future add rather than an initial pick.
- **MDT (Medtronic), DHR (Danaher), TMO already in** — MDT/DHR device/tools conglomerates too diffuse for a clean AI-attribution read; excluded to keep the basket legible.
- **Pre-revenue thin/unsponsored OTC AI-health names** — excluded by the US-listed, Fidelity-holdable liquidity mandate.
