# Nuclear Renaissance — Supply Chain Map

**Structure:** Long-only. All candidates US-listed common stock or full NYSE/NASDAQ listings (Fidelity-holdable mandate). Walk: AI power demand → restarts/uprates/new reactors → components & services → fuel (pounds → conversion → SWU). The further upstream and the fewer the licensed suppliers, the higher the priority.

## Bucket 1 — Fuel Cycle: Enrichment & HALEU (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
The bet: SWU is the hardest-to-substitute input in the entire theme. One US-owned enricher exists; HALEU for advanced reactors barely exists outside Russia. Centrus signed a $900M fixed-price DOE contract (2026-06-30) to build the first new US-owned commercial enrichment capacity at Piketon, atop a $2.4B LEU backlog.

| Ticker | Company | Exposure | Note |
|--------|---------|----------|------|
| LEU | Centrus Energy | Enrichment / HALEU | Sole US-owned enricher; DOE HALEU anchor contract + $2.4B LEU backlog; the purest bottleneck in the theme |

## Bucket 2 — Fuel Cycle: Mining, Milling & Conversion (HIGH PRIORITY)
The bet: ~180 Mlb/yr reactor demand vs ~130 Mlb primary supply, with Kazatomprom guiding 2026 output down and Cameco cutting McArthur River guidance. Bias to producing pounds; one development-stage kicker.

| Ticker | Company | Exposure | Note |

| UUUU | Energy Fuels | US conventional milling | White Mesa is the only operating conventional US uranium mill; rare-earth line dilutes purity but adds policy support |
| NXE | NexGen Energy | Development (Rook I, Athabasca) | Speculative: world-class undeveloped deposit; licensing timeline has slipped — sized as optionality, not cash flow |

## Explicitly Excluded

- **Kazatomprom (KAP)** — the world's largest producer, but London/Astana listing only; fails the Fidelity-holdable / no-local-only-listing mandate (same rule that excluded Keyence from robotics).
- **Urenco, Orano, Holtec, TerraPower, X-energy, Kairos, Westinghouse** — private or state-owned. Much of the theme's value pools here; this is the Vehicles Risk, not a reason to buy bad proxies.
- **DNN (Denison), EU (enCore)** — redundant with existing picks: DNN duplicates NXE's pre-production Athabasca exposure with less scale; EU duplicates UEC's US ISR exposure with weaker liquidity.
- **BW (Babcock & Wilcox)** — the right industrial heritage, wrong balance sheet: heavy debt and going-concern-grade distress; the theme doesn't need financed-to-the-edge vehicles. (verify current status before any reconsideration)
- **LTBR (Lightbridge), ASPI (ASP Isotopes)** — pre-revenue fuel-technology stories without the DOE anchor contracts that justify Centrus; ASPI additionally carries short-report/credibility overhang. Speculative sleeve is already full with SMR + OKLO.
- **Kept-but-diluted reference names — VST, D, GEV:** included for breadth but explicitly diluted. VST and D carry real nuclear fleets buried inside gas/regulated generation; GEV owns the lead Western SMR order book inside a diversified giant. All three are held behind the pure-play bottleneck names and scored down on theme exposure — they are the diluted-vehicle reference, not the core bet.
