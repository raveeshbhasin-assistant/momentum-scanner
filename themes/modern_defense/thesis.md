## The Future I'm Betting On

Modern defense is going through a once-in-a-generation restructuring driven by the battlefields of Ukraine, the Red Sea, and the Middle East: cheap autonomous systems are out-killing exquisite platforms by 10 to 100x on a cost basis. A $500 FPV drone destroys a $2M tank. A $50K loitering munition takes out a $30M air-defense radar. The legacy defense complex was built around small numbers of exquisite platforms (F-35s, Burke-class destroyers). The new reality demands massive numbers of cheap autonomous systems, plus the sophisticated, non-kinetic counter-systems to defend against them.

Expected revenue source weighting for tracker names over the 2-4 year horizon will be heavily international: **~50% European** (Rheinmetall artillery ramp, Saab anti-armor), **~30% allied non-Europe** (Israel, Indo-PACOM partners), and **~20% U.S.** (baseline DoD and the Drone Dominance Program). Names with concentrated allied exposure will be weighted highest in our scoring.

## Why Now (The Catalyst)

The lessons learned in Ukraine have officially graduated from emergency supplemental spending to baseline defense doctrine, but the execution speed varies globally. The near-term cash flow anchor is **European rearmament**, with €800B+ committed over the next five years to rapidly backfill depleted sovereign arsenals. In the U.S., the procurement pipes have finally opened: the Pentagon's **$1B Drone Dominance Program (DDP)** is currently running competitive "Gauntlets" with the explicit goal of fielding over **300,000 low-cost attack drones by 2027**. The demand signal has shifted from theoretical whitepapers to active, fixed-price delivery orders.

Furthermore, the most thesis-aligned sub-category — **High-Power Microwave (HPM) and Directed Energy (DE) counter-drone tech — has minimal clean public expression**. The best-positioned company (Epirus) is private. We will have to express this sub-thesis through the diluted exposure of LHX, BAESY, or RTX, accepting the suboptimal vehicle expression to capture the trend. The pure-play public landscape is littered with highly dilutive, cash-burning SPACs that cannot survive DoD audits. To survive the rigorous capital structure requirements, we must pick names with proven defense revenues and deep backlogs.

## Vehicles Scope: Global by Necessity

Because the U.S. public-market pool for clean defense-tech exposure is shallow and heavily diluted by legacy aerospace programs, our search must be global. European primes (Rheinmetall, Saab) and allied defense contractors (Elbit Systems via Israeli ADR) are essential components of the candidate universe. Europe is aggressively restocking its own arsenals, and allied nations often field battle-tested asymmetric tech faster than U.S. legacy primes.

## What I Expect the Market to Underestimate

- **The Counter-Drone (C-UAS) Economics:** It is mathematically unsustainable to shoot down a $2,000 drone with a $2 million Patriot missile. The market underestimates the massive structural demand for non-kinetic counter-drone tech — specifically high-power microwave (HPM) systems, directed energy, and specialized electronic warfare (EW) jamming gear that can defeat swarms for pennies per engagement.

- **The Decade-Long Munitions Restocking Cycle:** The West has depleted its stockpiles of 155mm artillery, propellants, and smart munitions. The market is pricing this as a temporary surge, underestimating the multi-year, locked-in contracts required just to return NATO arsenals to baseline pre-2022 readiness.

- **European Defense Electronics:** As European nations pivot from reliance on U.S. security umbrellas to domestic sovereignty, localized providers of advanced targeting sensors, radars, and drone payloads are seeing structural, un-cancellable backlog growth.

## What I Expect the Market to Over-Hype

- **SPAC Drone Startups & Hardware Micro-Caps:** The market loves the narrative of nimble startups disrupting defense, but most publicly traded drone SPACs (like RCAT, ONDS) will bleed cash and fail to meet the DoD's grueling scaling requirements. _(Note: We explicitly exempt AVAV from this category, as it is the one battle-tested survivor with massive fielded DoD revenue.)_

- **Legacy Air Defense as a Growth Story:** Bidding up RTX because it sells Patriot missiles ignores the reality that these exquisite kinetic systems are exactly what the new asymmetric drone doctrine is rendering economically obsolete.

- **"AI-Powered" Battlefield Software Pure-Plays:** Unless software is directly tied to a hardware platform or backed by a massive prime, standalone "defense AI analytics" startups face agonizingly slow sales cycles.

## Geopolitical Sensitivity

This thesis carries a unique structural risk: **it can gap down violently on peace**. Unlike AI data centers or space infrastructure, this theme is entirely dependent on global threat levels. A formalized armistice in Ukraine or a verified diplomatic reset in the Taiwan Strait could trigger an immediate **40-50% worst-case drawdown** across the pure-play sector as algorithmic trading prices in the evaporation of supplemental spending. **We will use a 30% drawdown as a hard trim-trigger.** This bet requires extreme discipline regarding entry points and a willingness to cut exposure on diplomatic breakthroughs.

## What Would Make Me Wrong

- **Exquisite Platforms Win the Lobbying War:** Entrenched defense primes successfully lobby Congress to kill funding for cheap autonomous swarms (like the Drone Dominance Program) to protect their highly profitable legacy programs.

- **Electronic Warfare Renders Autonomous Swarms Useless:** Near-peer adversaries deploy broadband EW and GPS-spoofing so effectively that attritable drones become completely useless, forcing a pivot back to heavily shielded, crewed platforms.

- **Antitrust and Consolidation Caps:** If traditional defense primes engage in an M&A spree and acquire **two or more of our top 5 tracker names at premiums below 40%**, the underlying thesis "wins" but public equity vehicles yield capped, suboptimal returns. _(40% threshold reflects typical defense-prime acquisition premiums of 35-50%; below 40% signals primes are absorbing value before public investors capture the upside.)_

- **Sustained U.S. Government Budget Disruption:** A severe, prolonged U.S. government shutdown or a return to rigid budget sequestration caps completely freezes new procurement contracts across the DoD.
