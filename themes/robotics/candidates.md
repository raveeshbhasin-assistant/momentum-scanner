## Bucket 1 — Machine Vision & Sensors (up-stack moat · Structural)

**CGNX — Cognex** (Nasdaq). Cleanest US machine-vision pure-play, now the load-bearing name. Q1 2026 revenue $268M, **+24% YoY**; gross margin **71.1%** (+420bps YoY); operating margin 22.3% (was 12.1%). New embedded AI-vision launches (In-Sight 6900 on NVIDIA, 3900 on Qualcomm) are the live proof of the "AI software upgrade to installed base" thesis. Risk: **P/E ~77** — richly valued; the "right thesis, watch the entry" name. 2026 guide is only mid-to-high-single-digit growth, so +24% Q1 sets a high bar.

**ZBRA — Zebra Technologies** (Nasdaq). Machine vision (Matrox) + dominant warehouse scanning/mobility installed base. FY26 guide 9-13% sales growth, $900M FCF target; AI/RFID/machine-vision pivot. Caveat: the machine-vision sub-segment is *currently in a cyclical downturn* (EV/semi manufacturing weakness) even as the company grows. Cleaner balance sheet and far cheaper than CGNX. Spans Bucket 1 and 2.

**Keyence** (KYCCF, unsponsored OTC). Global best-in-class — ~50%+ operating margins, dominant share. **Restricted vehicle:** thin OTC, wide spreads, FX. Track as the bucket benchmark but likely cannot size a position. _If a sponsored ADR / direct-Tokyo access opens, revisit._

**QCOM — Qualcomm** (Nasdaq). Edge-AI silicon; powers CGNX's In-Sight 3900. Launched Dragonwing IQ10 robotics processor (CES Jan 2026) for AMRs and humanoids. Cheap edge-AI exposure (low-teens P/E) vs NVDA. Partial-exposure / optionality name.

**NVDA — NVIDIA** (Nasdaq). Jetson edge + Isaac robotics stack — the VLA enabler. Richly priced, likely already an AI DC tracker name. Cross-theme reference, not a fresh robotics position.

## Bucket 2 — Mature Automation & Integrators (installed-base AI upgrade · Structural)

**ROK — Rockwell Automation** (NYSE). Cleanest US structural vehicle. Raised FY26 guide to ~$8.9B revenue, 5-9% organic; Q2 adj EPS $3.30 (+32%); enterprise operating margin 21.5%; **double-digit order growth** on reshoring + data-center/semi demand. $2B 5-yr capacity investment. Risk: trades near analyst targets.

**ABB** (NYSE ADR, liquid). Robotics + electrification; FY rev ~$34B, $1B buyback. **Catalyst: 100% spin-off of the Robotics division to a separate listing Q2 2026.** The one foreign name that holds cleanly. Diversified, so robotics is a minority of revenue today.

**Fanuc / Yaskawa** (FANUY / YASKY, unsponsored OTC). Global factory-robot and motion leaders. Restricted vehicles — track as benchmarks; size only if access improves.

**PH — Parker Hannifin** (NYSE). Motion & control, drives, actuators. Strong but **~$905 vs ~$512 fair-value** — expensive; wait-for-pullback. Spans Bucket 2/3.

## Bucket 3 — Mechanical Components (TARIFF TRADE only · Optionality ~20%)

**SYM — Symbotic** (Nasdaq). AI warehouse automation. **Now GAAP-profitable** — Q2 FY26 net income $9M (vs −$10M), revenue $676M (+23%), adj EBITDA $78M, **$2.0B cash, $218M FCF, $22.7B backlog.** Healthier than the demotion implied. **But ~85% Walmart concentration — and Walmart now owns part of its robotics supply.** Bucket 3, sized small, concentration named. Keep (not drop), don't promote back to structural.

**MOG-A — Moog** (NYSE). Precision electric/hydraulic actuators — a **US-listed, holdable** proxy for the actuator layer the Japanese names dominate. Record backlog, aerospace/defense/industrial demand; P/E ~43. The cleanest way to express the mechanical-components/tariff bet without a blocked Tokyo name.

**Harmonic Drive / Nabtesco / SMC** — **Blocked/Restricted.** The literal reducer bottleneck names, not cleanly holdable. The vehicles-wrong scenario made concrete. Express the tariff trade through MOG-A / PH instead, and **monitor HD/Nabtesco margin guidance as the Bucket-3 exit signal** even though we don't hold them.
