## Time Horizon & Strategy

Instead, we heavily short the "losers" of mass GLP-1 adoption (snacking, CPAP machines, dialysis) while taking long positions in the downstream lifestyle beneficiaries and the small-molecule API contract manufacturers that will scale the new oral pills.

## The Future I'm Betting On

For the last three years, GLP-1 adoption was throttled by the physical limitations of manufacturing peptides and assembling auto-injector pens, keeping prices high and access limited. That era is over. The approval of a true, highly effective once-daily pill that requires no refrigeration or fasting rules means distribution can scale instantly. The TAM is no longer "diabetics and the wealthy" — it is the broader 40%+ of the U.S. population with obesity. When 30-50 million people fundamentally reduce their caloric intake and curb impulsive cravings, the underlying economics of the junk food, orthopedic surgery, and sleep apnea industries break.

## Why Now (The Catalyst)

The catalyst is the April 2026 FDA approval of Lilly's Foundayo (orforglipron). While previous oral options (like oral semaglutide) had strict fasting rules and lower efficacy, orforglipron demonstrated ~12%+ weight loss in Phase 3 (ATTAIN-1) with zero food/water restrictions. At a cash-pay price of $149/month (as low as $25/month with commercial insurance) and broad telehealth + LillyDirect availability, the friction of needles, supply shortages, and insurance battles has been removed. The mass-market phase begins now.

## Vehicles Risk

The central risk is "Thesis right, LLY/NVO capture all the value." The demand-destruction thesis requires the market to accurately re-price consumer staples and med-tech stocks based on volume declines. If the market treats food volume declines as "temporary macroeconomic weakness" rather than permanent GLP-1 demand destruction, our short positions will face painful rallies, even as LLY marches toward a $2T valuation.

## Vehicles Scope: Strict U.S. Liquidity

Because we are heavily utilizing a short strategy, liquidity and borrow costs are paramount. We are restricting all candidates to U.S.-listed entities and high-volume ADRs. ## What I Expect the Market to Underestimate

- **The Velocity of Med-Tech Reductions:** The market assumes bariatric surgery, CPAP machines for sleep apnea (ResMed), and continuous glucose monitors (Dexcom) will see a "slow tapering" of demand. I bet oral GLP-1s will be prescribed as a first-line preventative defense, causing the pipeline for these severe interventions to dry up almost immediately.

- **The "Craving" Destruction:** GLP-1s do not just shrink stomachs; they modulate dopamine. The market underestimates the downstream hit to high-margin, impulse-buy categories like sugary snacks, alcohol, and fast-food transactions.

## What I Expect the Market to Over-Hype

- **Injectable Supply Chain (Auto-Injectors):** The market still views companies like West Pharmaceutical Services (WST) and Stevanato as picks-and-shovels monopolies. The pivot to oral solid-dose manufacturing will strand the massive capital expenditures these companies made to scale glass syringes and auto-injector pens.

- **The GLP-1 Mega-Caps (LLY/NVO):** They won the race, but their multiples leave absolutely zero margin for safety. A single political drug-pricing mandate or generic patent challenge could cause a 20% multiple compression, making them terrible risk-adjusted vehicles.

## Regulatory & Political Sensitivity

With tens of millions of Americans poised to take these drugs daily, GLP-1s are a massive target for Medicare negotiation and populist drug-pricing caps. The threat of forced price controls is exactly why we avoid going long the manufacturers directly, relying instead on the second-order volume effects that happen regardless of who is paying for the pill.

## What Would Make Me Wrong

- **The GI Side-Effect Wall:** Real-world adherence to oral orforglipron plummets because the daily gastrointestinal side effects (nausea, vomiting) prove intolerable outside controlled clinical trials, forcing patients back to weekly injectables or off the drugs entirely.

- **Severe Long-Term Safety Signal:** A Black Box warning emerges after millions of new users adopt the drug (e.g., acute pancreatitis, muscle wasting, or thyroid tumors), halting the supercycle overnight.

- **Food Companies Pivot Successfully:** The junk food conglomerates successfully re-formulate their portfolios to high-protein, GLP-1-friendly products, preserving their margins and completely breaking the short thesis.
