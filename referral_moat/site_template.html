The Referral Moat

Companies whose customers recruit their next customers should grow faster and more profitably than peers. Referral rate isn't a reported line item — so this scores its financial fingerprints: new revenue per sales dollar, falling sales intensity, steady compounding, pricing power, operating leverage.

Flywheel picks
