3. MAJOR - wf1_run.py level_stats L240-251, verdict L425-427.
   What: the WF-1/WF-2 p-value that enters Holm, and the whole AMBER path for level cells
   (`soft = mean > 0 and same_b and holm_p < 0.10`), rest on raw excess over SPY. The control-adjusted
   p (`cp`, L244) is computed and discarded. Only GREEN looks at controls (ctl_lo > 0).
   How it bites: the event sample is mostly 2026 survivors of an equal-weighted small/mid-cap
   universe (finding 1). Random eligible survivor names have a non-zero mean excess over SPY for
   reasons unrelated to insiders (survival conditioning, size tilt, period). If that baseline is
   positive in both CONF_A and CONF_B, WF-1 reaches AMBER with no insider effect at all; if it is
   negative in CONF_A (mega-cap-led SPY), a real effect is masked. The "same sign in B" gate for level
   cells inherits the same baseline and is close to vacuous.
   Smallest fix: make control-adjusted excess the tested statistic for WF-1/WF-2 (p = max of quarter
   and day cluster on ctl63), keep SPY excess as a reported gate; require ctl mean > 0 (and same sign
   of ctl mean in B) for AMBER. Report the selftest level mean as the artefact baseline.

4. MAJOR - boot_mean / boot_diff, level_stats L241-243, diff_stats L263-264.
   What: clusters are entry quarters (and entry days) resampled as independent. A 63-session hold is
   one quarter long, so every event's return window lies partly (on average half) in the NEXT
   quarter: adjacent quarter clusters share return days and the common small-cap-vs-SPY factor. Day
   clusters overlap even more, so the quarter interval is the binding one and it is too narrow.
   Second, there are only 26 quarter clusters in CONF_A (24 in DISC) and they are very unequal: WF-1
   CONF_A registered events per quarter run 1 / 88 / 253 (min / median / max); one quarter holds 11%
   of all events. A percentile bootstrap on ~25 unequal clusters under-covers.
   How it bites: entries late in 2020Q1 and early in 2020Q2 ride the same rebound; the bootstrap
   counts that as two independent confirmations. Intervals and p-values are optimistic by an amount
   that grows with the common-factor share of the excess.
   Smallest fix: add a third scheme and take the widest of three: moving-block bootstrap over
   consecutive quarters with block length 2 (or half-year clusters). State it in the prose. Using
   control-adjusted excess as the statistic (finding 3) also removes most of the common factor.

5. MAJOR - evaluate L198-209 with PREREG "Known limits" (controls are 2026 survivors only).
   What: "conservative for a long rule" holds for a level, not for a difference between arms. The
   survivor-only control pool is biased upward by an amount that depends on the state: in DOWN /
   after-drop states many names later die, so the surviving controls are the ones that recovered and
   their mean is inflated far more than in the UP state. Events include (some) delisted names.
   How it bites: WF-3 (UP minus DOWN, two-sided, control-adjusted): the DOWN arm's control-adjusted
   excess is pushed down more than the UP arm's, so the difference is pushed toward "UP is better"
   with no insider effect. WF-4 is pushed against its hypothesis (drop arm penalised), WF-6 is
   roughly neutral (both arms survivor events). The favoured-arm gate limits but does not remove a
   false WF-3 pass.
   Smallest fix: pre-register that each difference cell must also hold with the same sign on
   survivor-only events (events and controls then carry the same conditioning), and report both; or
   test WF-3/WF-4 on SPY excess and control-adjusted excess and require agreement.

6. MAJOR - build_events.py L46-52, L71 (`match_rate`, `match_n` from the issuer's full trade history),
   wf1_run.py qualifying L124.
   What: a series is accepted on trades dated after the event as well as before, and on split
   factors dated after the event (raw prices are rebuilt from every later split). It is the only
   filter in the engine that reads post-event information.
   Scale: among officer/director filings >= $25k, rate < 0.70 removes 15% of delisted-issuer filings
   (689 of 4,575) but 3.5% of survivor filings (2,140 of 61,567). match_n < 5 is small today (176
   filings) but the 46k not-yet-fetched filings are the low-activity issuers where it will bind.
   How it bites: (a) an issuer with a wrong or missing later reverse-split record fails the match for
   its pre-split trades and all its events vanish; reverse splits follow bad outcomes. (b) a
   short-lived issuer whose insiders made 3 trades and then the company failed never reaches 5
   trades; the same event in a company that lived on and accumulated sales passes. Both drop bad
   outcomes preferentially on the side of the sample that carries the survivorship correction.
   Smallest fix: compute match_rate / match_n per event from trades with trans_date <= that event's
   filing date (expanding), keep the 0.70 / 5 thresholds; report events removed by each cause per
   year (see finding 1b). If n < 5 point-in-time, fall back to requiring the event's own trades to
   price inside the range (px_ok_share already does this).

7. MAJOR (false-negative direction) - level_stats L247-254, gate `top5_share <= 0.30`.
   What: the share is top-five names' summed excess divided by the NET sum of all excess. Net sum =
   n x mean, while the top five of several hundred 63-session small-cap excess returns sum to
   several hundred percent regardless of the mean.
   How it bites (arithmetic, assumed not measured: top-5 sum ~ 4 to 6, i.e. 400-600%): n = 300 with a
   true mean of +3% gives a net sum of 9, share ~ 45-65%: FAIL. Passing needs n x mean >= ~15-20,
   i.e. about 700 events at 3% or 1,000 at 2%. The gate therefore overrides the registered n >= 300
   and makes GREEN on WF-1/WF-2 depend mostly on n. CONF_A WF-2 has 1,366 registered events BEFORE
   the $5 / $10M eligibility cut.
   Smallest fix: gate on `mean_ex_top5` (already computed) having a bootstrap lower bound > 0, or
   define the share against the gross sum of positive excess. Decide before the lock, not after DISC.

D. VERIFIED CLEAN (item 1 checks and others) - and how
====================================================================================================

- Signal day s vs filing date vs entry: sidx() = last session <= filing date; evaluate enters at s+1.
  Checked on all 154,919 rows: entry_date == cal[s+1], entry_date > filing_date in every row, 0 NaT.
  99.8% of filing dates are sessions. Form 4 filing dates include submissions up to 22:00 ET the same
  day, all public before the next open. Calendar runs to 2026-10-02, so a 2026-04-01 entry has its
  full 63 sessions.
- Rolling windows: adv (20), sma (200), 252-high, r20 (shift 20), dark-flow s5 / z / sane are all
  trailing and indexed at s (code reading). r20 cannot be NaN for an eligible name (sma needs 200
  consecutive closes), and both NaN comparisons with DROP resolve to False. float32(-0.15) <= -0.15
  is True.
- Raw (as-traded) price for the $5 floor: AAPL 2020-08-28 raw_close 499.23, close 124.81, f = 4:
  the split un-adjustment is right; later splits are used only to restore the price as traded.
- Returns: ao = Open x Adj/Close and acf = ffilled Adj; holding period is open of s+1 to close of
  s+63 = 63 sessions; `trunc` agrees between survivor (last < s+h) and delisted (x < e+h-1) paths;
  SPY is measured over the identical span in both paths.
- last_bar: written by build_events, never read by the engine (grep).
- Refractory: strictly backward on filing_date, applied on the full history before the window
  filter, so the event set does not depend on window edges.
- Cluster definition: window is [d_i - 30d, d_i] on filing dates, backward only; same-day filings
  are included and are public before the next open. Dedupe keeps the earliest filing.
- WF-5 sign file: written only when window == DISC and not selftest; CONF runs only read it.
- entry_date window filter: datetime64[us] against the string bounds works under pandas 3 (run via
  make_events output); event frames built from lists of rows keep datetime/int/bool dtypes.
- routine: lookup is (owner, year-1..3, month): backward by transaction date (caveat: finding 9).
- 8-K window: only filings with filing_date <= Form 4 filing date are counted: no look-ahead
  (caveat: finding 22). news8k_events.csv cik is int64, matching d.cik.
- FMP delisted bars: close is split-adjusted (median |log jump| at 60 split bars 0.03) and volume
  looks split-adjusted (no volume step with the split ratio), so raw_volume = volume / f is right.
  Fetch window is first filing - 400d to last filing + 400d: enough history for the 200-session
  filters and no artificial truncation inside a 63-session hold.
- ADV decile for delisted events: at most one rank off the panel's definition; immaterial.
- darkflow.pkl: unique (date, symbol), all dates on the calendar, short <= total everywhere.
- Holm implementation (step-down with running max, NaN -> 1) is correct. NW(4) Bartlett weights
  cover the 21-session / 5-session overlap (4 lags). boot_diff resamples quarters jointly for both
  arms. Boolean masks in run() (nn, cov, sup, zj) are built from d row by row: lengths match.
- verdict() gates match the prose for level, difference and WF-5 cells except findings 3, 17, 24.
