## 2. Fill and cost realism

- Tiered cost as registered is the PRIMARY line: 5 bp/side if `adv20_dollar >= 1e8`, 10 bp/side if `2e7 <= adv20_dollar < 1e8`. Flat 10 and flat 15 bp/side are MANDATORY sensitivity lines for every cell. For R4 ($20-100M names, +3% gap day, 10:05 fill) 10 bp is optimistic: half-spread on a $20-40 gapper is commonly 5-10 bp on its own before impact. A rule that passes at tiered cost but not at flat 15 bp is reported as "passes at registered cost only".
- Net R = `(exit - E - E*2*bps/1e4)/(E - stop)`; net pnl% = `(exit/E - 1)*100 - 2*bps/100`.
- Entry fill = `O` of the entry bar (yfinance 5m Open = first print; can be stale/odd-lot). Skip the signal (count it as "skipped") if `O_E <= stop` or `O_E >= target` at the time of entry; NO re-arm, no later attempt on that ticker-day.
- Stop fill, for each bar j from the entry bar onward: if `O_j <= stop` -> exit at `O_j` (gap-through), elif `L_j <= stop` -> exit at `stop`. Target: if `O_j >= target` -> exit at `O_j`, elif `H_j >= target` -> exit at `target`. Both barriers touchable in the same bar (and not resolved by the open) -> LOSS. Intrabar stop fills at exactly the stop price are optimistic on fast gappers; the 15 bp line is the only cushion, so it must be shown.
- Per-capital: equity fixed at $100k, no compounding; risk = 1% of equity per position -> `shares = 1000/(E - stop)`; ADDED notional cap = 25% of equity per position (with 1-2% stops the uncapped rule implies 50-100% notional per name and 250-500% gross with K=5, which is not a realistic personal account). Report capped (primary) and uncapped. K = CONCURRENT cap (the JSON key `K_per_day` is a misnomer; plan 3.4 says concurrent); a slot freed by an exit inside bar j is available to entries at bar j+1 or later. K=5 primary, K=10 secondary, applied WITHIN a cell; cells are never pooled.
- No 12:00-13:00 entries anywhere (plan 3.3); the R2 window is set so this never binds (see table).

## 3. Survivorship

- `bars1d_all` and therefore `inplay_candidates.csv` and the 5m download list derive from a symbol list as of 2026-09-13. Names delisted between 2024-09 and 2026-09 (bankruptcies, buyouts, reverse-merger shells) are absent.
- U3 direction: UPWARD bias for long rules (bankrupt names removed; buyout targets removed which is roughly neutral-to-slightly-negative for the breakout style). With ADV >= $50M and Close >= $5 the bias is smaller than in micro-cap studies but not zero, and it is LARGER in the older folds (more time for delisting to occur). SPY is survivorship-free, so "excess over SPY" inherits the full bias.
- U1 direction: SLIGHTLY FAVOURABLE to the rules. The gappers most likely to be missing over a 3-month window are cash-takeover targets (gap up, then pinned to the deal price: tiny ATR -> 0.5%-floor stops -> noise stop-outs, i.e. small losers under S_a). Magnitude is bounded by the number of such names, expected to be about 1-2% of watchlist ticker-days.
- Locked bounding for U3: (a) run on names with >= 480 daily rows in the window AND report the share of names excluded by that cut and their trade count (these are IPOs/spin-offs, i.e. survivors, so the cut does not remove bias but removes warm-up artefacts); (b) report every W-cell per fold AND for the subset `adv20_dollar >= 2e8` (where delisting risk is lowest); (c) recommended (not part of the registered pass) robustness guard: a genuine effect should not decay toward the present, so flag any W-cell whose excess in the last 2 folds is <= 0 as "survivorship-suspect".
- Locked bounding for U1: report, per day, (i) watchlist size before/after the 5m-coverage cut, (ii) names in the top-20 without a 5m file. No back-filling.

## 4. Ambiguities closed (validator may not choose)

- Bar labels: START time. "10:00 bar close" is known at 10:05:00; "enter 10:05 open" = `O_{10:05}`.
- Windows: all inclusive at both ends in bar-start terms as written in the table. R2 candidate signal bars start in [10:00, 11:50] so the entry bar starts <= 11:55 (no 12:00-13:00 entries). R3 candidate signal bars start in [09:45, 10:50] so the entry bar starts <= 10:55 ("before 11:00").
- "First trigger" = the earliest bar in the window satisfying all conditions; if its entry is skipped (open beyond a barrier) or its entry bar is missing, the ticker-day is done for that cell. One position per ticker-day; no re-entry.
- Comparisons: `>=` where the text says "at least/>=", strict `>` where it says "above". R1: `C_{10:00} >= 1.005*O_{09:30}` (which subsumes "> RTH open") AND `C_{10:00} > VWAP_{10:00}`. R2: `L_b <= VWAP_b AND C_b > VWAP_b AND C_b > O_b` (literal registered text; a "reclaim from below" qualifies; adding a prior-bar-above-VWAP precondition would be a NEW variant and must go to things_tried.csv). R3: `C_b > ORH AND C_b > VWAP_b`, strict.
- Opening range = the three bars 09:30, 09:35, 09:40 (closes 09:45). `ORH = max H`, `ORL = min L` of those three; all three must exist.
- Ties in the K queue: R0/R1/R4/R5 signals all carry the SAME signal time (10:05:00). Locked: ties are broken at RANDOM; the per-capital result is the mean over seeds 0..199 with the 5th-95th percentile band across seeds. A gap-rank tie-break may be shown as a labelled secondary line only. Per-pick statistics ignore K entirely.
- Per-pick avgR = POOLED pick-weighted mean of net R (the DISC script used mean-of-daily-means; both are reported, the pooled mean is the decision metric). CI = day-cluster bootstrap resampling days with replacement, recomputing the pooled mean, >= 2000 reps, seed 20260913, 95% percentile.
- Two-sided bootstrap p per cell = `2*min(P(mean<=0), P(mean>=0))`; Holm at alpha 0.05 across m = 11 as registered (10 enumerated rule x structure cells + 1 pad; the registration's count of 11 does not match the 10 enumerable cells and the pad is the conservative resolution). Every extra variant evaluated increases m by one.
- "Excess over benchmark > 0" and "effect not equal in size in the benchmark": locked as rule net avgR minus benchmark net avgR >= +0.05R for BOTH benchmarks (0.05R is one cost-model unit at a 2% stop; anything smaller is inside modelling error). CI of the excess is reported (day-cluster bootstrap on paired daily differences) but the registered CI requirement applies to the rule's own net avgR only.
- Random-bar benchmark: for each rule trade with entry bar E on day d, draw a name uniformly from W_d and a bar uniformly from the three bars of the 15-min bucket containing E (buckets start 09:30, 09:45, ...); enter at that bar's Open with the same structure (ATR and day-low at the bar before it). Seeds 0..199; report the mean.
- min_picks 300 and min_days 25 apply to PER-PICK counts and to distinct signal days. R4/R5 (subsets of R1) are LIKELY to fall below 300 in a 29-trading-day TEST window (max possible R0 count is 20 x 29 = 580). A cell below the minimum is reported "INCONCLUSIVE (n=...)", never "failed" and never rescued by lowering the threshold or pooling DISC+TEST.
- U3 folds: 8 = [2024-09-15..2024-12-31], 2025Q1, 2025Q2, 2025Q3, 2025Q4, 2026Q1, 2026Q2, [2026-07-01..2026-09-10]; a trade belongs to the fold of its ENTRY date. Quarter-cluster CI with 8 clusters is anti-conservative; the month-cluster bootstrap (24 clusters) is ALSO required and both lower bounds must exceed 0 (stricter than registered, therefore admissible).
- U3 dedupe: one open position per ticker at a time; a new signal while a position is open is ignored (not queued). Per-trade statistics (the registered pass metric) use ALL qualifying trades subject only to W2's registered 10-new-entries/day cap (ranked by RS = ret20 - ret20_SPY desc, tie -> adv20_dollar desc). The 10-concurrent / equal-dollar portfolio is reported separately and is NOT the pass metric. No stops in swing cells (none registered).

## 7. Expected failure modes

- R4 and R5 (subsets of R1) fall short of n >= 300 in 29 trading days -> INCONCLUSIVE, with pressure to pool DISC+TEST or relax the minimum. Forbidden.
